📋 Today's lesson

Tick the sets you are using today – for example a song, a story and a poem – then tap Play. All their words go into one deck for every game. A word that is in more than one set is only used once.

Tap a picture box to choose a picture. On the Mac you can also drag a picture onto a row, or drop several pictures anywhere to make new words (the file name becomes the word). Changes save automatically.
